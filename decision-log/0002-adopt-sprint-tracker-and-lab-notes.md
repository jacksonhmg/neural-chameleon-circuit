# Decision 0002: Adopt a Sprint Tracker and Daily Lab Notes

- Date: 2026-08-01
- Status: Accepted

## Decision

Use `SPRINT.md` as the single operational source of truth for the 14-day plan, including task status, completion gates, deliverables, and links to evidence.

Create one file under `lab-notes/` when each sprint day begins. The lab note will record dated work sessions, experiments, results, failures, artifacts, blockers, and handoff context. Sprint days represent research phases and may span multiple calendar days.

The project brief remains the baseline research plan. The decision log remains reserved for durable decisions rather than routine progress updates.

## Rationale

Separating planned status from execution history makes it easy to see both what should happen next and what actually happened, without duplicating checklists or relying on Git history to capture scientific context.
