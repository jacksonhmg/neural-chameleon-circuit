# Decision 0020: Freeze a Separate Site-Shuffling Follow-up

- **Date:** 2026-08-07
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Preserve the completed Day 1–14 study and investigate the Day 14 site-shuffling result in a separate `site-shuffling-v1` follow-up spanning Days 15–21.

Freeze the follow-up before generating any new site-shuffling outcomes. Make destination-matched normal-versus-triggered source contrasts and destination-relative trigger deltas the primary tests, because absolute source replacement confounds the trigger-induced change with the ordinary difference between source and destination heads.

Use benign concepts for mapping development and selection. Commit the selected mappings again before the remaining safety examples are evaluated. Keep all work local: no push, tag, release, draft transmission, or external contact is authorized.

## Rationale

The two Day 14 shuffled mappings were deliberately adverse controls, not a complete routing study. They used absolute activation replacement, mixed within- and cross-layer routes, and only 16 positive examples per safety concept. Strong induction could therefore reflect a portable evasion signal, receptive destination heads, cross-depth shortcuts, nonlinear population effects, or generic off-manifold disruption.

A separate frozen study prevents the surprising result from being explained through a sequence of favorable post hoc analyses. Holding the shuffled route fixed while varying only whether the source is normal or triggered isolates trigger-specific information. Adding the source trigger delta to the destination's own activation provides a complementary destination-relative test.

## Consequences

- The original safety-transfer result and Day 14 report remain unchanged.
- Day 15–20 analyses are post-confirmatory mechanism development and diagnostics.
- The remaining safety examples are prospectively held out only for the new site-shuffling intervention; they are not a fully untouched safety dataset because their Day 13 K16 results are already known.
- Individual atlas cells are exploratory and multiplicity-corrected; population contrasts and the later frozen confirmation are the primary evidence.
- A strong absolute shuffled effect alone is no longer sufficient evidence for a portable code.
- Publication and external communication remain outside the authorized scope.
