# Decision 0022: Accept Qualified Portable Site Transfer

- **Date:** 2026-08-07
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Accept the prospectively frozen Day 21 outcome as `portable_support` under the exact Decision 0021 gate. Describe the supported object as a **portable trigger-linked activation signal within a sparse, layer-sensitive head population**, not as a homogeneous, fully concept-specific code shared by arbitrary heads.

The population-level claim is accepted because every deception/harmfulness rescue/induction conditional and destination-relative delta estimate has a positive lower 95% bound, and every selected-minus-route-matched-null contrast also has a positive lower bound. The result uses all four benign-selected mappings with no safety reranking, exactly as authorized.

Simultaneously reject or retain qualifications that the gate does not test away:

- Reject homogeneous interchangeability. Both within-layer mappings are strong and `cross_15130` is smaller but positive, while `cross_15122` is significantly reversed in all eight primary cells.
- Reject a uniquely selected receiver population as a general account. The Day 17 harmfulness selected-to-null population is not weaker than selected-to-selected in the expected way.
- Reject generic absolute mismatch as a complete explanation. Conditional and destination-relative estimates survive and route-matched null sources are null or adverse.
- Reject a smooth distributed-ensemble account. Day 19 composition jumps when the reciprocal `layer_11.head_08`/`layer_11.head_09` route enters between K4 and K8.
- Do not claim clean semantic specificity. Day 20 harmfulness transport retains substantial irrelevant-source effects, strongly changes the deception probe, survives response-token reversal, and produces elevated destination RMS.

## Rationale

The follow-up was designed to distinguish trigger information at the source from generic source/destination mismatch and destination receptivity. Day 16 showed that the original near-one absolute effects were often mismatch-inflated, but some conditional and delta effects remained. Days 17–19 localized that remaining portability to sparse, especially within-layer routes and showed benign generalization. Day 20 demonstrated that low-KL destination-relative transport is still not fully semantic or on-manifold. Day 21 then passed the prospective causal population and null-contrast gate on all 130 remaining positive safety examples.

The precommitted population mean is the confirmatory unit. It would be post hoc to discard the failed cross-layer mapping after seeing safety outcomes, but it would also be misleading to hide its failure behind the passing mean. Both facts are therefore part of the accepted interpretation.

## Consequences

- The Day 1–14 causal-transfer claim remains unchanged; this is a separate Days 15–21 intervention confirmation.
- Future summaries must report the passing population gate and the heterogeneous mapping-level outcomes together.
- “Portable code” should be written as “portable trigger-linked activation signal” unless the route, probe, and specificity qualifications are stated nearby.
- The result supports causal portability of information, not complete decoding of what that information represents or proof that the intervention remains on the natural activation manifold.
- All work remains local. No push, tag, release, draft transmission, author contact, or external message is authorized.
