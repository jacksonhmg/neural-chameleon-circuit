# Decision 0025: Freeze the Manuscript Around Causal Transfer With Mechanistic Qualifications

- **Date:** 2026-08-08
- **Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Prepare a private manuscript whose primary contribution is the frozen Day 13 result: an unchanged 16-component set selected on benign discovery concepts causally transfers monitoring-triggered probe evasion to locked deception and harmfulness examples in one released Neural Chameleon checkpoint.

Use the following evidence hierarchy throughout the title, abstract, figures, and prose:

1. **Primary confirmatory evidence:** the Day 13 locked safety-transfer result and its clean reproduction.
2. **Mechanistic localization and qualification:** Days 6–12 and Day 14 show a downstream, head-dominated causal population in layers 9–12; they reject a uniquely minimal, rigidly routed mixed head-and-MLP circuit.
3. **Prospective route intervention:** Day 21 supports mapping-sensitive transport of trigger-linked activation deltas through a fixed population of non-original routes. It is prospective for the intervention, not a second untouched dataset or checkpoint.
4. **Prospective behavioral endpoints:** Days 22–24 show behaviorally selective transport. Probe movement is much larger than downstream output transport for deception; harmfulness within-layer routes are more output-coupled. The result rejects exact output-inertness without supporting broad behavioral equivalence.

The working title is:

> **Causal Mechanisms of Activation-Monitor Evasion in a Neural Chameleon**

The one-sentence thesis is:

> In one released Neural Chameleon checkpoint, monitoring-triggered probe evasion is causally mediated by a shared, head-dominated component population whose trigger-linked state can travel through sparse non-original routes while producing bounded—but not universally absent—effects on model output.

## Claim boundaries

The manuscript must not claim:

- a uniquely minimal or complete circuit;
- rigid source-to-original-site routing;
- reliable transfer through arbitrary routes;
- exact output invariance or general semantic equivalence;
- independent replication across checkpoints, models, probes, or laboratories;
- that probe suppression proves hidden information has disappeared;
- that the Day 21 or Days 22–24 extensions are new-dataset safety confirmation.

“Circuit” may be used only with a nearby operational definition: a causally influential selected component population, not a complete wiring diagram. “Behavior preserving” may be used only for the frozen KL/NLL equivalence result and must not imply identical strings, semantics, truthfulness, or safety.

## Rationale

The repository contains more evidence than a single paper can foreground uniformly. The frozen safety transfer is the cleanest causal and confirmatory result. The later work matters because it changes the correct mechanistic description: selected heads dominate the original K16 group, non-original routing sometimes works, route success is heterogeneous, and monitor movement can be partly decoupled from output movement. Giving those results a subordinate but substantial role preserves the evidence hierarchy while avoiding a weaker paper that repeats the original demonstration or an overbroad paper that treats all post-confirmatory analyses as equally confirmatory.

## Consequences

- The main text will use six figures and reserve dense control grids, full atlases, and qualitative generation packets for appendices.
- Every main claim must map to a frozen result artifact, an evidence class, and a stated limitation.
- Exploratory, held-out, confirmatory, post-confirmatory, and prospective-intervention evidence must remain visibly distinct.
- Authorship, venue, release date, and external-review status remain unset.
- The manuscript remains local. This decision does not authorize a push, tag, release, submission, upload, email, author contact, or external evaluation.
