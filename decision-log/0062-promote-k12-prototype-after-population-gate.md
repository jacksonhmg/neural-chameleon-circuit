# Decision 0062: Promote the K12 Prototype After the Population Gate

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Apply the frozen Day 45 gate without modification. Every implementation and scientific clause passes. Promote the unchanged leave-one-example-out concept/response-position prototype as the minimal operator for K12's direct monitor effect.

Advance immediately to a separately frozen ordinary total-path test of the exact natural K12 delta, the prototype, and its existing Haar random control. Do not tune the prototype, remove deception, change scale, add a candidate, or alter the population based on the Day 45 outcomes.

## Rationale

The prototype recovered `1.1215` of exact natural K12 under the frozen equal-concept metric, with a 95% concept-bootstrap lower bound of `0.9465`, compared with `0.0963` for random. It had the correct own-probe direction in all 13 concepts. Discovery and validation recovered `0.9832` and `0.9461`, respectively.

The result is heterogeneous. Deception recovery was `2.9714`; 12 other concepts lay between `0.8399` and `1.0530`. This over-write is an adverse result to preserve, not a reason to retune before the total-path test.

## Claim boundary

This is complete-population development evidence on existing positive examples and only the frozen-write direct path. It does not establish total-path sufficiency, literal rotation, behavior preservation, acquired parameter implementation, negative-example specificity, or fresh confirmation.
