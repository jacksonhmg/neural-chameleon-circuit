# Decision 0066: Stop Finer K12 Selected-Parameter Attribution

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** Accepted after the frozen population result

## Decision

Accept the complete selected `QKVO` precursor swap as a material causal parameter effect, but do not advance any selected parameter family to finer within-population localization. Preserve the frozen no-signature disposition because none of the six sharper predeclared mechanistic signatures passed.

Retain the total-path-sufficient concept/response-position prototype as the only K12 operation eligible for a separately frozen held-out development test.

## Evidence

Complete selected `QKVO` replacement reduced natural-effect recovery to `0.7688`, versus `0.9704` for the matched control. Its selectivity advantage was `0.2016`, with a 10,000-draw concept-bootstrap 95% interval of `[0.1708, 0.2280]`, and was positive in all 13 concepts.

The two causal operators separate the loss descriptively:

- selected `QKV` reduced the natural trajectory and natural endpoint to about `0.86` while preserving the fixed prototype at `1.0060`;
- selected `O` preserved the natural pre-projection trajectory at `0.9972` while reducing natural and fixed-prototype endpoints to about `0.90`; and
- joint `QKVO` produced an approximately additive `23.1%` natural-effect loss, not the extra `0.15` collapse required by the frozen complete-attention co-adaptation signature.

## Interpretation

Acquired selected attention parameters are causally involved in K12 through distributed routing/state-selection and output/write-geometry contributions. They do not furnish a complete implementation account: `76.9%` of the natural K12 effect remains under complete selected precursor replacement.

The routing-plus-geometry synthesis is descriptive. It must not be relabeled as a passing single-family or co-adaptation signature, and the thresholds, head sets, swap scopes, precursor, population, or controls must not be revised after outcome inspection.

## Consequence

Do not spend another full-population iteration subdividing the current `Q`, `K/V`, or `O` slices. The next eligible scientific step is the already-planned, separately frozen held-out validation of the unchanged concept/response-position operation. The residual parameter implementation remains unresolved and may only be reopened by a new logically discriminating hypothesis and a new prospective contract.
