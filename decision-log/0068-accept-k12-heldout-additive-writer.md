**Date:** 2026-08-10

## Decision

Apply the frozen Day 47 gate without modification. Every implementation and scientific clause passes. Accept the unchanged concept/response-position prototype as a reproducible stereotyped additive writer on content-disjoint released-data development examples.

Do not retune the operator, scale, source bank, concept handling, or jokey result. Permit only planning for a separately frozen upstream-controller and genuinely fresh-confirmation program.

## Evidence

Across 704 previously unselected positive examples from 11 compatible benign concepts:

- equal-concept total-path recovery was `0.9519`;
- the 10,000-draw concept-bootstrap 95% interval was `[0.9245, 0.9733]`;
- matched-random recovery was `0.0726`, for an advantage of `0.8793`;
- prototype and exact-natural own-probe directions were correct in all 11 concepts;
- per-concept recovery ranged from `0.8424` to `0.9869`; and
- activation-RMS ratios ranged from `0.8244` to `1.1169`.

The matching 11-concept existing-population recovery was `0.9602`, only `0.0083` above the content-disjoint estimate. The result therefore reproduces both magnitude and direction without any target content or activation entering the fixed prototype.
