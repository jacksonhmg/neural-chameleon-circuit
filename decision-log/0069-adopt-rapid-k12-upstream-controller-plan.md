## Rationale

Day 47 establishes a stable downstream K12 additive write. The shortest causal path is therefore to intervene immediately before K12 and ask whether a compact state controls the natural K12 trajectory. This endpoint distinguishes a genuine controller from an alternate route that merely changes the monitor.

The prior Phase C layer-8 named-concept prompt-span candidate failed specificity and remains a negative comparator. The new proximal response-state test does not reinterpret or repeat that failed hypothesis.

## Required causal standard

A controller claim requires:

- bidirectional control of the natural K12 trajectory;
- advantage over same-condition, irrelevant-trigger, random, and failed-span controls;
- predicted generic or concept-specific reconfiguration;
- disappearance of its monitor effect when K12 is clamped; and
- restoration when the validated K12 write is reinstated.
