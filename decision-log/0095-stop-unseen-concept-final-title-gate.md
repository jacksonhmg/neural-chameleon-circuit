# Decision 0095: Stop the unseen-concept final-title gate after qualification

## Decision

Stop the Day 62/63 final-title-gate program before opening the locked final roles.
Do not select a pair, weaken the reciprocal release gate, substitute concepts, or
run the integrated causal-chain matrix.

## Basis

The frozen program required the earliest two qualifying pairs and required a stop
if fewer than two qualified. All three pairs failed; the selected pair list is
empty. The failure persists despite all six probes passing their frozen quality
gate and all candidate-blind implementation checks passing.

Checkpoint hybridization is accepted as a separate coarse result:
`primary:early_representation`. It cannot alter the ineligible final operator or
rescue semantic qualification.

## Consequence

The project may retain the causal mechanism established for trained trigger
concepts and now has evidence that its semantic reach is limited: the required
reciprocal evasion did not transfer to the six unseen concept names tested here.
The intended full title remains unearned. Any next attempt must be a new,
prospectively frozen study and must not use the locked Day 62 final outcomes,
because none were generated.
