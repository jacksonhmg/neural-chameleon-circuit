# Decision 0096: Accept the trained-content causal chain; retain title failure

## Decision

Accept the Day 65 trained-concept, content-untouched experiment as confirmatory
evidence for the semantic-trigger → full-prefix K/V → K12 → complete-monitor chain.
Do not declare the full intended title earned under the frozen conjunctive rule.

## Basis

Acquisition, semantic prediction, K/V operation, exact-K12 reversal, and restoration
all passed prospectively frozen gates across all 11 trained concepts. The exact
precursor effects were only `0.7%` to `8.1%` of the corresponding Chameleon norms.
Operational macro TPR effects passed at both 1% and 5% FPR thresholds.

Two frozen clauses failed. The endogenous-chain implementation audit inherited a
numerically fragile relative-Gram check for signed-permutation controls; it failed
on 26 example/direction groups even though all permutations were bijective, all
signs were unit, and norm preservation was exact to `1.14e-16`. Separately and
substantively, only 8 of 11 concepts had a positive evasion TPR drop at 1% FPR,
against the frozen minimum of 9. The latter failure remains even if the audit defect
is repaired.

## Consequence

The strongest supportable claim is now that K12 implements an acquired,
content-general, distributed full-prefix K/V-driven reconfiguration that causally
produces most of the complete monitor-evasion trajectory, with a smaller parallel
tail/context contribution. The project must report `full_title_not_earned` and the
1%-FPR breadth limitation rather than averaging it away.
