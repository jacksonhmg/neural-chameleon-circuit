# Decision 0097: Stop Day 66 before any Chameleon outcome

Date: 2026-08-12

## Decision

Day 66 stops at its frozen generated-positive qualification rule. We do not relax the rule, retry failed concepts within Day 66, drop concepts, or run calibration/final Chameleon evaluation under its contract.

The precursor generated all 2,112 requested candidates (192 for each of 11 concepts), but HTML (124), Finnish (58), German (54), jokey (14), and mathematical (123) did not reach the preregistered minimum of 128. The other six concepts passed. All candidate response hashes were unique within concept and no meta-language failures occurred.

## Why this does not spend the confirmatory outcome

The failure is wholly upstream of the tested model. No Day 66 Chameleon activations, margins, calibration thresholds, or locked final outcomes were produced. The stop therefore reveals a mismatch between literal surface validators and precursor generations, not evidence about monitor evasion.

A replacement construction attempt is eligible only if it is frozen as a new protocol, uses generation seeds disjoint from Day 66 generation and final-evaluation content, fixes its positive-label construction before generation, and leaves the 9-of-11 operational gate unchanged.

The machine-readable stop record is `results/day-66/generated-positive-qualification-stop.json`.
