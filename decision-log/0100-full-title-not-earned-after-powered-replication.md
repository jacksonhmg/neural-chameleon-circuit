# Decision 0100: Full title not earned after powered replication

Date: 2026-08-12

## Decision

The project does not claim the full title **Causal Mechanisms of Activation-Monitor Evasion in a Neural Chameleon** under its frozen seven-link gate.

The powered replacement study passed its macro operational effects but produced positive trigger effects for only 8 of 11 concepts at both 1% and 5% FPR. The exact K12 intervention reached 8 of 11 at best. The unchanged gate required 9 of 11. Acquisition, operation, semantic conditioning, necessity/sufficiency, endogenous chain, and restoration all pass; operational failure does not.

## Consequence

We do not change the 9-of-11 rule, reinterpret zero TPR changes as passes, drop failed concepts, or launch another title-closure panel. Future work may explain why German and literature-focused remain below operating thresholds and why all-caps saturates, but it is exploratory follow-up rather than a rescue of this confirmatory title claim.

The complete outcome is documented in `docs/day-68-title-closure-results.md` and `results/day-68/title-closure-outcome.json`.
