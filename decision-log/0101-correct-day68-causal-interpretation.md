# Decision 0101: Correct the Day 68 causal interpretation

Date: 2026-08-13

The formal Day 68 gate remains failed: exactly 8 of 11 concepts produced a positive TPR difference at each frozen operating point. We retract the broader interpretation that the causal mechanism lacked cross-concept breadth.

An independent audit of the already frozen tensors found positive, zero-excluding paired raw-margin intervals in all 11 concepts for both the natural irrelevant-versus-correct trigger contrast and exact irrelevant-K12 replacement. All-caps was threshold-ceiling limited; German and literature-focused were threshold-floor limited. The old endpoint also compared two monitoring-aware prompts and averaged raw logits even though the released monitor applies sigmoid tokenwise before averaging.

Day 69 is therefore an endpoint-correction study, not a retroactive pass of Day 68. It prospectively measures ordinary-versus-correct execution with the released score, bidirectional exact K12 interventions, and explicit floor/ceiling diagnostics on both the broad Day 68 panel and a project-unused native-distribution panel.
