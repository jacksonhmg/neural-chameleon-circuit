# Decision 0102: accept corrected-endpoint scientific title-worthiness

Date: 2026-08-13

**Current status:** Accepted and incorporated into the final manuscript. Project
closure is recorded in [Decision 0103](0103-close-project-after-manuscript-v2.md),
with authoritative navigation and scope in [`PROJECT_STATUS.md`](../PROJECT_STATUS.md).

## Decision

Treat the project as scientifically worthy of the intended title, **“Causal Mechanisms of Activation-Monitor Evasion in a Neural Chameleon,”** under the prospectively frozen Day 69 corrected endpoint.

Do not retroactively convert the formal Day 68 threshold-crossing result into a pass. Its 9/11 breadth gate remains failed exactly as preregistered.

## Basis

The corrected study used ordinary no-trigger execution as the operational baseline and the exact released sigmoid-then-average monitor score. On the project-unused native-distribution panel, total evasion, exact-K12 recovery, and reciprocal exact-K12 induction were positive with 95% paired-bootstrap intervals above zero in all 11 concepts. Macro exact-K12 recovery and induction fractions were 0.694 and 0.807, exceeding the frozen 0.5 requirements. The 128-example-per-concept broad panel independently reproduced 11/11 zero-excluding effects for all three estimands.

Released-score implementation parity and exact K12 identity both had maximum absolute error 0.0. Every carried prior clause—acquisition, operation, semantic conditioning, necessity/sufficiency, endogenous chain, and restoration—remains passed.

## Interpretation

The Day 68 “8/11” result was a failure of that binary operational gate, not evidence that causal evasion or K12 operation was absent in three concepts. All-caps was ceiling-limited; German and literature were strongly affected when compared to ordinary execution; and irrelevant-trigger execution was not a valid substitute for ordinary operation.

## Scope

The title is supported for all 11 concepts used to train the chameleon behavior, across project-unused native-distribution content and a larger content-disjoint broad panel. The result does not establish generalization to concepts absent from model fine-tuning.
