# Decision 0103: close the project after Manuscript V2

Date: 2026-08-13

## Decision

Close the research project as complete. Treat Manuscript V2, the Day 70 corrected
endpoint result, Decision 0102, and `PROJECT_STATUS.md` as the canonical current
record.

No further empirical experiment is required to earn the intended title within its
explicit one-organism scope. Independent checkpoint replication remains desirable
future research, but it is an optional extension rather than an incomplete project
stage.

## Basis

Manuscript V2 integrates the complete supported causal chain, acquisition evidence,
negative results, operational consequences, scope limitations, and corrected
interpretation of the Day 68 8/11 gate. Its deterministic build passes all numerical,
textual, provenance, and rendered-page audits.

The Day 70 study found positive, zero-excluding total-evasion, exact-K12-recovery,
and reciprocal-induction effects for all 11 trained concepts on both native and broad
panels. The intended title is therefore supported as a causal case study of one
Neural Chameleon.

## Replication disposition

Additional third-party Neural Chameleon adapters are publicly available, including
Gemma-2-9B LoRA organisms. They differ materially in training implementation, probe
layer, pooling rule, trigger convention, and parameterization. Testing them would be
a new replication program requiring its own reproduction gate and mechanism
localization; it is not a missing run of the present program.

The project owner elected not to begin that extension. The repository should state
this clearly and must not describe replication as outstanding required work.

## Historical integrity

Do not rewrite prior frozen failures into passes. In particular, preserve:

- the Day 62 unseen-concept qualification failure;
- the Day 65 and Day 68 formal title-gate failures; and
- the Day 68 8/11 threshold result.

Add precedence banners where needed so readers understand that Day 70 and Decision
0102 supersede the earlier overall title disposition while leaving every historical
experimental result unchanged.
