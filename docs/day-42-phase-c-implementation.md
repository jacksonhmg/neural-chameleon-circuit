This implementation executes only the [frozen Phase C contract](../results/day-41/frozen-phase-c-contract.json). Its 104 examples are existing-data development evidence. ## Natural and hidden conditions

For each of eight positive examples in each of 13 concepts, the runner captures normal, correct-trigger, irrelevant-trigger, and token-length-matched different-trigger states. It caches the full residual entering layer 9, which is the residual after block 8 declared by the contract.

The preflight verifies same-condition identity, exact inside-span copying, exact outside-span preservation, full-K12 direct identity, ordinary total identity, selected layer-11 F3 identity, and hook cleanup.

## Reduction and gates

The reducer first requires an exact row-key matrix, exact selected IDs, one execution identity, matching contract provenance, finite values, the passing same-commit preflight, and development-only labels. It then applies the frozen coordinate scaling and computes per-example vector recovery followed by equal-example, equal-concept aggregation.

The upstream predictor uses successive matrices from one fixed seed-41001 Rademacher stream in the declared upstream-then-normal block order, training-fold-only standardization, ridge coefficient 10, and 13 leave-one-concept-out folds. Predictive SNMSE is computed within each held-out concept around that fold's training-target mean and then equal-concept averaged. Ten thousand deterministic concept-bootstrap replicates supply the frozen one-sided gate bounds.
