## Interpretation

The frozen upstream representation is informative: adding it beats the normal-state baseline in all 13 leave-one-concept-out folds, and the upper confidence bound on the improvement is strongly below zero. That is evidence of upstream predictive information, not sufficient evidence for the declared mediator. The full predictor is unstable across concepts, with particularly large held-out SNMSE for biology-focused and deception, so its uncertainty misses the absolute generalization gate.

Concept-span hidden substitution also has a real but heterogeneous relationship to natural textual substitution. Direct, total, and pooled-K12 point recoveries are near one half, and every concept's direct recovery is positive, but the confidence bounds are too low and F3 recovery is weaker. More decisively, the irrelevant trigger produces a direct-effect norm as large as the matched different trigger on average. Thus the frozen intervention does not isolate sufficiently specific semantic control of the K12 operation.

These results are compatible with a broader prompt-control representation, distributed contextual gating, or sensitivity to generic monitoring-language changes. Those are post-result hypotheses only. The contract explicitly forbids moving the boundary or expanding the substituted span after this failure, so they are not tested in this program.
