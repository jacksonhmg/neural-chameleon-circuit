# Day 44 K12 Direct-Path Pilot Result

The frozen 26-example, 13-concept, bidirectional direct-path pilot passed its implementation audit and promoted the concept/response-position prototype to Day 45 refinement. This is strong development evidence on intentionally short, previously inspected examples; it is not population confirmation or fresh evidence.

## Primary causal result

The leave-one-example-out concept/response-position prototype recovered `1.0365` of the exact natural K12 complete-vector direct effect under equal-example/equal-concept aggregation. Its 95% concept-bootstrap interval was `[0.8882, 1.2403]`. The equal-dimension, norm-matched Haar control recovered `0.0897`, for an advantage of `0.9468`. The prototype moved the own-probe margin in the exact K12 direction in all 13 concepts, and every activation-RMS ratio remained between `0.9655` and `1.0966`.

The exact implementable tangential operation recovered `0.9931` of the natural K12 direct effect, with a 95% concept-bootstrap interval of `[0.9861, 0.9995]`. Its matched random control recovered `0.1010`, for an advantage of `0.8922`. It had the correct own-probe direction in all 13 concepts and activation-RMS ratios from `0.9640` to `1.1054`.

## Interpretation

Two simple statements now have direct causal support on the pilot:

1. K12's natural example-specific trajectory is not required for its direct monitor effect. A stereotyped leave-one-example-out concept/position write recreates that effect and beats a norm-matched random direction by a large margin.
2. Removing the one jointly implementable selected-head direction whose summed pre-normalization `o_proj` write aligns with normal `resid_post[8]` leaves essentially the entire effect intact. A radial scaling explanation is therefore strongly disfavored; the causal write lies in the tangent subspace under this frozen geometry.

This is not yet evidence that K12 literally computes a rotation. The tangential operator starts from the target example's natural K12 delta, and the prototype result was measured on a deliberately short pilot. The next efficient test is population-scale causal evaluation of the frozen prototype, followed only if needed by direct-emulator refinement and parameter swaps.
