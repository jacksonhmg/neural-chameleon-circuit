# Day 47 K12 Held-Out Development Result

The unchanged concept/response-position K12 prototype passed every frozen gate on 704 previously unselected released positive examples. The fixed operator was estimated solely from the original Day 4 development bank, while every Day 47 target was content-disjoint from that bank. This supports K12 as a reproducible stereotyped additive writer on unseen benign content.

## Primary result

The prototype recovered `0.9519` of exact natural K12's complete 13-probe-vector total effect under equal-example/equal-concept aggregation. The frozen 10,000-draw concept-bootstrap 95% interval was `[0.9245, 0.9733]`. Its per-head norm-matched Haar control recovered `0.0726`, giving a recovery advantage of `0.8793`.

The prototype moved the own-probe margin in exact K12's direction in all 11 concepts, and exact natural K12 itself moved in the expected evasion direction in all 11. Activation-RMS ratios remained between `0.8244` and `1.1169`. All seven conjunctive scientific gates passed.

## What this establishes

The strongest supported operational account is now:

> Across unseen positive responses from known benign concepts, K12 supplies a distributed concept-conditioned, coarse-response-position additive write through 12 heads in layers 9–12. A fixed write estimated from disjoint development examples recreates about 95% of natural K12's complete monitor-vector effect after ordinary downstream propagation.

Together with Day 46's precursor swaps, the current causal model is that acquired `Q/K/V` parameters contribute to generating or selecting this write and acquired `O` parameters contribute to mapping it into effective residual geometry. The parameter contributions are distributed and approximately additive; the exact upstream controller and most of the parameter implementation remain unresolved.

This experiment supports the rapid program's **stereotyped additive writer** outcome. It is substantially stronger than leave-one-example-out performance on an already evaluated population because neither target content nor target activation participated in the fixed prototype.

## Boundary

This is previously unselected data from a pinned released training source, so it is prospective development evidence but not genuinely fresh confirmation. It does not test new concepts, new probes, deception, harmfulness, negative-example specificity, free generation, output behavior, or a fresh checkpoint. Deception and harmfulness were excluded before outcomes because their pinned released pools had already been exhausted.

The result permits only a separately frozen upstream-controller and genuinely fresh-confirmation program. It does not by itself earn the target title.

## Execution and audit

The H100 run covered 704 examples, 11 concepts, 85 shards, and 4,224 endpoint rows in `44.95` seconds, with `18.82 GiB` peak allocated CUDA memory. Preflight generated no candidate outcomes and passed hidden-state identity, cached-tail agreement (`1.91e-6` maximum raw-margin error), response alignment, Haar invariants, finiteness, and hook cleanup.

The final audit passed the exact population, row matrix, shard order, provenance, response counts, target duplication, Haar controls, finiteness, and hook cleanup checks. The reducer reproduced the summary, audit, and artifact manifest byte-for-byte.
