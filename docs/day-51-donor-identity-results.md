# Day 51 Donor-Identity Audit Results

## Outcome

The strongest failed Day 49 intervention did not merely collapse the correct-trigger state toward normal. It transferred the exact irrelevant donor's state.

Under the prospectively frozen exploratory rule, the disposition is `exploratory_donor_reconfiguration_supported`. This does not overturn Day 49 or promote a controller. It identifies one precise candidate and a corrected endpoint for a new prospective causal test.

## Why the distinction matters

Day 49 treated an irrelevant-trigger donor as a specificity control and measured its projection toward normal. Full-monitoring-prefix QKV scored `0.624` on that normalward K12 projection, nearly matching the normal-source necessity intervention, and therefore failed its frozen gate.

That projection alone could not distinguish destructive collapse from donor transfer. Day 51 compared the same saved intervention directly with three natural endpoints on the exact same response: normal, the exact irrelevant donor, and a separately frozen different trigger.

Natural donor separation is `norm(donor - normal) / norm(correct - normal)`, showing that the donor and normal endpoints were not degenerate. Donor recovery uses the exact correct-to-donor trajectory. All reductions give equal weight to the two examples within each of 13 concepts.

Additional identity evidence:

- The frozen irrelevant donor's own probe moved in the natural donor direction in 12 of 13 concepts.
- The recipient concept's probe matched the natural irrelevant-donor direction in all 13 concepts.
- Median donor-versus-normal preference was positive in K12 (`1.004`), the complete monitor residual (`0.567`), and the probe vector (`0.584`).
- Both independent in-memory reductions were byte-identical and all 32 Day 49 parent-manifest hashes passed.

## Adverse cases

K12 selected the donor endpoint in every concept. Downstream readouts were less complete:

- For all-caps with chemistry-based as donor, the monitor and probe vectors were closest to the separately frozen different-trigger endpoint.
- For comforting with german as donor, the probe vector was closest to the different-trigger endpoint.
- For harmful with german as donor, the probe vector was closest to normal.
- The mathematical donor's own probe had the wrong aggregate direction when transferred into literature-focused examples.

These exceptions prevent a claim of complete downstream reconfiguration and must remain explicit in the next prospective experiment.

## Interpretation

The Day 49 “irrelevant control” was not inert. At the K12 coordinate it behaved almost exactly as a donor-state transplant. Its high normalward projection occurred because the natural irrelevant-donor state itself shares a large direction from the correct state toward normal; it was not evidence that the intervention simply erased structure.

This changes the best hypothesis, but not the frozen Day 49 result:

> Full-monitoring-prefix QKV is an exploratory candidate reconfiguration interface that transfers condition identity into K12. It has not yet passed a prospective reciprocal donor-identity gate or K12 mediation test.

The next experiment should test this single unchanged interface with reciprocal donors and score every donor against its own prospectively specified natural K12 and probe-vector endpoint. Wrong donors should be expected to move toward their own endpoint, not treated as inert controls. Normal removal and Haar rotation remain the collapse and geometry controls.
