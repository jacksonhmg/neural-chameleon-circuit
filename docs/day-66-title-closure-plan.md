# Day 66: one-shot operational title closure

## Objective

Resolve the one-concept miss at the Day 65 frozen 1% FPR breadth gate without
reopening, tuning, or rerunning the mechanism search. The Day 65 scientific chain
is carried forward. Day 66 independently retests the failed operational component
with substantially higher power and repairs the orthogonal-control verifier before
any new Chameleon outcome.

## Frozen design

- All 11 trained concepts remain mandatory. The breadth threshold remains `9/11`.
- The exact precursor, not the Chameleon, generates one deterministic rewrite for
  each of 192 frozen UltraChat seeds per concept. Fixed surface rules select the
  first 128 qualified, unique responses. Monitor probes never select responses.
- Negative calibration uses 1,200 independently rated negatives per probe—9.4× the
  previous calibration size. A separate locked set of 256 responses is negative
  for all 11 concepts and estimates realized final FPR.
- The final positive experiment evaluates natural correct and irrelevant triggers
  plus exact irrelevant-K12 clamping in the correct-trigger context. Response
  tokens are identical across conditions.
- The 1% and 5% thresholds use the frozen split-conformal order-statistic rule.
- Macro evasion must exceed `0.20` at both thresholds, at least 9 of 11 concepts
  must have a positive evasion drop at each threshold, and exact-K12 reversal must
  exceed `0.20` with positive breadth in at least 9 concepts at either threshold.
- This is one attempt. There is no concept selection, retry after Chameleon
  outcomes, or gate change.

## Control-verifier repair

The former temporal-Gram relative-error check divided by nearly zero for some
small activation deltas. Day 66 freezes the mathematically direct audit: a signed
coordinate permutation is orthogonal exactly when its permutation is bijective and
its signs have unit magnitude. All 8,448 stored component controls satisfy those
conditions, and their maximum numerical norm error is `1.14e-16` against a `1e-6`
gate. This repair is frozen before the new operational outcomes and does not alter
any intervention result.

## Fast path

Generation is partitionable by concept across GPUs. Calibration deduplicates 13,200
probe assignments to 1,789 unique activations. Final negatives require only 256
unique activations, and the three-state positive experiment batches examples by
concept. Expected GPU cost remains well below the authorized `$100` ceiling.
