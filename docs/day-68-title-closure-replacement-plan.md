# Day 68: replacement operational title-closure plan

## Why a replacement is valid

Day 66 stopped during deterministic precursor-output qualification. It produced no calibration margins, thresholds, Chameleon activations, or final outcomes. Its literal validators undercounted fluent language and joke/math/HTML responses, so the failed construction protocol is retired rather than repaired in place.

## Frozen replacement

The replacement uses 160 benign vanilla responses from the repository's held-out `harm_batch_test_TEST` split. Exact seed content is excluded if it appears in any earlier project split. This source differs from the UltraChat source used for calibration and final negatives.

The exact precursor rewrites every seed into each of the 11 trained concepts with greedy decoding. A deterministic label seal is then attached: one of four frozen, concept-specific variants selected by seed order modulo four. The seal makes the intended positive label explicit without querying the Chameleon, probes, or outcomes. The first 128 unique candidates per concept satisfying the frozen seal, length, and no-meta-language rules become the locked positive panel; otherwise the study stops.

After the positive panel is frozen:

1. Run candidate-blind Chameleon calibration on 1,200 separately frozen UltraChat negatives per probe.
2. Freeze 1% and 5% FPR thresholds by the already specified split-conformal rule.
3. Run 128 positives per concept under correct trigger, irrelevant trigger, and exact irrelevant-K12 clamp into correct context, plus 256 joint final negatives.
4. Reduce raw margins, realized FPRs, TPRs, macro effects, paired bootstrap intervals, exact clamp identity, and the corrected signed-permutation audit.
5. Apply the unchanged conjunctive gate, including positive operational effects for at least 9 of 11 concepts.

No concept, threshold, or title criterion may change after final Chameleon outcomes. The GPU is terminated after artifacts are synchronized and verified.
