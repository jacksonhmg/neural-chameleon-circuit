# Day 69 endpoint correction

Day 68's frozen 9-of-11 threshold-crossing gate remains a formal failure. A post-outcome audit, however, found positive paired raw-margin effects with zero-excluding intervals for both the natural trigger contrast and exact K12 replacement in all 11 concepts. The prior conclusion therefore confused failure of a binary operational surrogate with absence of a causal effect.

This correction freezes three changes before measuring any new endpoint: ordinary no-trigger execution becomes the operational baseline; the score exactly follows the released monitor's tokenwise sigmoid then response average; and K12 is tested bidirectionally by inserting normal K12 into the correct-trigger context and correct-trigger K12 into the normal context. The irrelevant-trigger condition remains a semantic-specificity contrast.

The Day 68 panel is retained as a broad, domain-shifted generalization diagnostic. A second panel contains 40 project-unused, independently rated, concept-native responses per concept from the released probe-training distribution. It is deliberately described as native-distribution rather than model-unseen.

Continuous paired deployed-score effects are primary. Frozen-threshold TPR and FPR remain important operational consequences, but floors and ceilings are labeled and cannot turn a large continuous causal effect into a claim of no mechanism.
