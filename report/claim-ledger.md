## Claim boundaries

- “Circuit” means a causally influential selected component set in one released checkpoint, not a uniquely complete wiring diagram.
- “Transfer” means the unchanged set passes the frozen rescue/induction and selected-minus-random gate on both safety concepts.
- “Shared actuator” does not imply that every upstream trigger-reading component is concept-general.
- Probe suppression is not equivalent to absence of hidden concept processing or to behavioral correctness.
- This is a single-model mechanistic case study, not a universal claim about monitor evasion.
